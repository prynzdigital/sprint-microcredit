Is this the exact amount I will pay?

It uses our current rate of {{site.calc.monthlyRatePct}}% per month, charged flat on the amount borrowed. Your exact rate, any fees and the total you repay are confirmed in writing before you sign.

How much of my salary should go to loan repayments?

At Sprint, your new loan repayment plus any loans you pay outside your payslip (MoMo, bank or app loans, hire purchase) should stay within {{site.calc.affordLimitPct}}% of your take-home pay. Take-home pay means your salary after tax, SSNIT and anything already deducted on your payslip. For example, on GH₵4,000 take-home pay with a GH₵500 MoMo loan, the new repayment should be no more than GH₵1,500 a month. The calculator is a guide; final approval is subject to the credit committee.

Does a longer repayment period cost more?

Usually, yes. A longer period lowers each monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay. Choose the shortest period you can comfortably afford.

What does “cost per GH₵100” mean?

It’s the interest you pay for every GH₵100 you borrow over the whole loan. It makes it easy to compare different amounts and repayment periods.
