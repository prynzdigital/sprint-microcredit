Ask on WhatsApp
Is this the exact amount I will pay?

It’s an estimate using an illustrative rate of {{site.calc.monthlyRatePct}}% per month. Your exact rate, fees and total repayable are confirmed in writing in your offer before you sign.

How much of my salary should go to loan repayments?

As a rule of thumb, keep all your loan repayments together under about 30% of your take-home pay, so you still cover rent, food, transport and savings.

Does a longer repayment period cost more?

Usually, yes. A longer period lowers each monthly payment but increases the total interest you pay. Choose the shortest period you can comfortably afford.

What does “cost per GH₵100” mean?

It’s the interest you pay for every GH₵100 you borrow over the whole loan. It makes it easy to compare different amounts and repayment periods.
