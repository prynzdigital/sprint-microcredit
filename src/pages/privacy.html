Privacy notice

Your trust matters. Here’s how we handle your information.

Who we are

{{site.legalName}} (“Sprint”, “we”) is a micro-credit company located at {{site.address.street}}, {{site.address.locality}}, Accra, Ghana. We handle personal data in line with Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

What this website collects

This website does not use a database or tracking forms. The application form on this site builds a message on your own device. Your details are only shared with us when you choose to send that message through WhatsApp or email.

The interactive tools (loan calculator, eligibility checker, budget planner and quizzes) run entirely in your browser. Nothing you type into them is sent to us.

Information you give us when applying

When you apply for a loan we may collect your name, contact details, Ghana Card details, a passport-sized photo, employment and salary information, payslips, bank statements, post-dated cheque details and your guarantor’s details. We use these only to assess and manage your loan, meet our legal obligations and contact you about your account.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We may share information with licensed credit reference bureaus, regulators, or service providers where required by law or necessary to provide your loan.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google Map on the Contact page. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook and LinkedIn take you to those services, which have their own privacy policies.
