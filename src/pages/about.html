Our story

Keeping our clients “Always Ahead”

Sprint Micro-Credit is a trusted, fast-growing lending institution dedicated to salaried workers from credible institutions, especially in times of financial urgency.

With a focus on speed, trust and customer care, we make short-term loans simple and stress-free, whether it’s an urgent need or a personal goal. We stand out not just for our competitive interest rates, but for the values we uphold.

The people behind your loan

Decades of banking, credit and people experience, focused on one thing: fast, reliable loans for salaried workers.

Chief Executive Officer · Founder

Samuel Gbor

From credit officer to regional and senior manager at several prestigious banks in Ghana, Samuel has earned his banking expertise every step of the way.

With over 18 years in banking and microfinance, he founded Sprint Micro-Credit to deliver fast, reliable loans to salaried workers and to set an unmatched standard in financial services. His leadership combines operational excellence with a passion for financial inclusion.
