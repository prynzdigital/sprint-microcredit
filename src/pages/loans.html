Requirements

Who qualifies

  • You’re 18 or older and a Ghanaian resident
  • You earn a regular monthly salary paid into a bank account
  • You work for a financial institution, an SOE, or another established employer
  • Your repayments will fit comfortably within your take-home pay

Documents to bring

  • Valid Ghana Card
  • Your most recent payslips (usually the last 3 months)
  • Bank statement showing your salary (usually 3–6 months)
  • Staff ID card
  • Your active phone number (and MoMo number, if applicable)

We may ask for one or two more items depending on your employer. We’ll tell you upfront.

FAQ

Loan questions, answered

Can I get a loan if I already have another loan?

Possibly. We look at your total monthly repayments compared to your take-home pay. If adding a Sprint loan keeps you within a comfortable range, we can help.

Can I repay early?

Yes. Talk to us about early repayment and we’ll confirm the amount to settle your loan.

How do I repay?

Repayments are made monthly, in line with your payday. We agree the repayment method and dates with you before disbursement.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Contact us immediately. Late payments may attract charges and can affect your credit record, but we always prefer to find a workable solution together.

I’m not with a financial institution or SOE. Can I still apply?

Yes. Send us your details. If you’re a confirmed staff member of an established employer and your salary is paid into a bank account, we’ll review your request.
