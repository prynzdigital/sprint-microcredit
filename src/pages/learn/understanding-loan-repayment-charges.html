Understanding Loan Repayment Charges: What Borrowers Should Know

By Sprint Micro-Credit3 min readPublished 15 Nov 2026

Taking a loan comes with financial responsibilities, and understanding the costs involved is an important part of borrowing responsibly. While borrowers often focus on the loan amount and interest rate, it is equally important to understand any additional charges that may arise during repayment.

One concern some borrowers have is the additional charge they encounter when making loan repayments through digital payment platforms.

Why are there additional charges?

When borrowers make repayments through digital payment platforms, transaction processing charges may apply. These charges relate to the processing of electronic payments and are separate from the actual loan repayment amount owed to the lending institution.

The applicable charge can depend on the payment method, transaction type and the fee arrangement in place with the payment provider. For this reason, borrowers should check the transaction details displayed before confirming a payment.

A payment-processing charge is not automatically additional loan interest or a fee imposed by the lender. If a charge is unclear, ask the payment provider or your lending institution to explain it.

What should borrowers do?

  • Understand the payment process: familiarise yourself with the payment channels available and any applicable transaction charges.
  • Check transaction details: review the amount displayed before confirming a payment.
  • Keep payment receipts: retain transaction confirmations as proof of payment.
  • Ask questions: if a charge is unclear, contact the relevant payment provider or contact us for clarification.

Final thoughts

Understanding every aspect of loan repayment helps borrowers plan their finances more effectively and avoid unexpected surprises.
