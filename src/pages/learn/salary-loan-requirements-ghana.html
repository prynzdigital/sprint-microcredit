Applying for a Salary Loan? Here’s How to Make the Process Easier

By Sprint Micro-Credit4 min readPublished 15 Nov 2026

Applying for a salary loan doesn’t have to be complicated. However, understanding the requirements and preparing the necessary documents beforehand can help make the process smoother.

If you’re considering applying for a salary loan, here’s what you should know about the process at Sprint Micro-Credit.

1. Prepare the required documents

Before beginning your application, ensure you have the following documents ready:

  • Six months’ recent bank statements: to provide an overview of your income and financial transactions.
  • Three months’ recent payslips: to help establish your employment income.
  • Ghana Card: for identification and verification purposes.
  • Passport-size photograph: for your application records.
  • Signed cheques: cheques covering the proposed loan repayment period, properly completed. (Why cheques are required)
  • Guarantor: a guarantor who meets the applicable requirements for the loan.

Note: the phone number you give us should be registered in your own name.

Having these documents ready can help reduce delays during the application process.

2. Affordability assessment

Once the necessary documents have been submitted, your financial information is assessed to determine whether the requested loan is affordable.

This assessment considers relevant income information, existing financial commitments (including loans not deducted on your payslip) and your ability to meet the proposed repayment obligations. How to check affordability yourself.

At Sprint Micro-Credit, loan applications are reviewed by the Risk Manager and the risk assessment team, who determine the appropriate loan amount based on their assessment. Final approval is subject to the credit committee.

Meeting the initial documentation requirements does not automatically guarantee loan approval.

3. Review and complete your loan documents

If your application is approved, a credit officer will bring the offer letter and loan application form to you.

Take time to read through the documents carefully before completing them. Ensure that you understand:

  • The approved loan amount.
  • The applicable interest and charges.
  • The repayment amount and duration.
  • Your responsibilities as a borrower.
  • Any other relevant loan conditions.

Complete the required sections and sign where necessary. If anything is unclear, ask for clarification before signing.

4. Document processing and disbursement

After completing and signing the necessary documents, they are returned to the office for processing.

The submitted documents are reviewed and processed as part of the final disbursement procedure. Once the necessary checks and processing have been completed, the approved loan amount is disbursed in accordance with the agreed arrangements.

Final thoughts

Being prepared is one of the simplest ways to make your loan application process easier. Ensure your documents are complete, provide accurate information and take time to understand the terms of your loan before committing.

At Sprint Micro-Credit, we aim to make accessing salary loans a straightforward and professional experience while ensuring that every application undergoes the necessary assessment.
